# Superstocks Investment Strategy - Detailed Plan

## 1. Stock Selection Criteria

### Technical Super Laws (8 Laws)

#### The Top 5 Must-Haves:

1. **Price Under $10**
   - Ideal range: $3-7 at initial breakout
   - Rarely consider stocks over $10
   - Lower prices allow for greater percentage gains

2. **The Magic Line Respect**
   - Stock must respect its 10-week simple moving average (10 WMA)
   - This is the ultimate support level
   - Stock should bounce off this line repeatedly
   - Some stocks may use 8 WMA, 12 WMA, or 14 WMA as their "magic line"
   - Identify through trial and error

3. **Powerful Base Formation**
   - Look for "staircase" patterns
   - Multi-month consolidation periods
   - Tightening price action before breakout
   - Volume dry-up during base formation

4. **Volume Surge on Breakout**
   - Minimum 50% above average volume
   - Preferably 100-200% above average
   - Sustained volume increase post-breakout

5. **Acting Like a Superstock**
   - Making new highs regularly
   - Holding above key moving averages
   - Strong relative strength vs market
   - Orderly pullbacks

### Fundamental Super Laws (12+ Laws)

#### Critical Requirements:

1. **Earnings Winner**
   - Accelerating quarterly earnings growth
   - Positive earnings surprises
   - Forward guidance raises
   - Path to sustained profitability

2. **Revenue Growth**
   - 20%+ year-over-year growth minimum
   - Preferably 50-100%+ growth
   - Accelerating revenue momentum

3. **Low P/E Ratio (Relative to Growth)**
   - Single-digit P/E ideal for profitable companies
   - PEG ratio under 1.0
   - Undervalued relative to growth rate

4. **Insider Buying**
   - Multiple insiders buying
   - Buying at increasing prices
   - Recent purchases (within 3 months)
   - Size of purchases relative to insider net worth

5. **Game-Changing Story**
   - Disruptive technology or product
   - Major contract wins
   - Regulatory approvals
   - Industry transformation potential

6. **Float and Share Structure**
   - Relatively small float (under 50M shares ideal)
   - Limited dilution risk
   - No significant overhead supply

#### Additional Firepower:

7. **No Listed Options** (reduces manipulation)
8. **Under-followed** (few analysts)
9. **Industry Leadership** potential
10. **"WOW" Factor** - something extraordinary about the company
11. **Sustainable Competitive Advantage**
12. **Strong Balance Sheet** (low debt, adequate cash)

## 2. Entry Strategy

### The 6 Super Laws of Low-Risk Entries

1. **Buy the Magic Line Hand-Over-Fist**
   - Primary entry: When stock touches 10 WMA
   - Best risk/reward entry point
   - Where institutions accumulate

2. **Buy Pullbacks in Uptrends**
   - 15-25% pullbacks from highs
   - Must maintain uptrend structure
   - Volume should dry up on pullback

3. **Buy Breakouts from Bases**
   - Multi-week/month consolidations
   - Breakout on high volume
   - Clear resistance level breach

4. **Never Chase Extended Moves**
   - Don't buy more than 20% above magic line
   - Wait for pullback to support
   - Patience is crucial

5. **Scale Into Positions**
   - Initial position: 25-33%
   - Add on confirmation: 33-50%
   - Final add near magic line: remainder

6. **Timing With Market**
   - Avoid entries in declining market
   - Best entries when market recovering from oversold
   - Monitor market breadth and sentiment

## 3. Position Management

### Position Sizing Rules

1. **Concentration Strategy**
   - Maximum 3-5 positions
   - 20-40% per position when confident
   - Up to 50% in highest conviction ideas

2. **Kelly Criterion Application**
   - Size based on edge and odds
   - Larger positions when all factors align
   - Reduce size in uncertain conditions

### Risk Management

1. **Stop Losses**
   - Mental stops, not hard stops
   - Generally 15-20% below entry
   - Close below magic line = warning
   - Two consecutive weekly closes below = exit

2. **Portfolio Heat**
   - Maximum 50% drawdown tolerance
   - Reduce exposure in weak markets
   - Move to cash when necessary

## 4. Exit Strategy

### The Super Laws of Selling

1. **Parabolic Move Exit**
   - 50-100% gain in 2-3 weeks
   - Exponential price acceleration
   - Euphoric sentiment
   - Massive volume spike

2. **Magic Line Violation**
   - Close below 10 WMA for 2 weeks
   - Failure to reclaim after test
   - Decreasing volume on bounces

3. **Fundamental Deterioration**
   - Earnings miss
   - Guidance reduction
   - Insider selling
   - Competition emergence

4. **Technical Breakdown**
   - Major support breach
   - Failed breakout attempt
   - Relative strength deterioration
   - Distribution patterns

5. **Time Stops**
   - No progress in 3-4 months
   - Opportunity cost consideration
   - Capital redeployment

6. **Market Conditions**
   - Broad market breakdown
   - Sector rotation away
   - Risk-off environment

## 5. Market Timing

### Entry Conditions

1. **Bullish Market Indicators**
   - Major indices above 50-day MA
   - Positive breadth
   - VIX under 20
   - Sector rotation into growth

2. **Sentiment Extremes**
   - Buy at pessimism extremes
   - Avoid euphoria periods
   - Contrarian indicators

### Exit/Cash Conditions

1. **Bearish Signals**
   - Major indices below 200-day MA
   - Breadth deterioration
   - VIX spike above 30
   - "Canary in the coalmine" indicators

2. **Personal Indicators**
   - Taxi drivers giving stock tips
   - Media euphoria
   - IPO frenzy

## 6. Research Process

### Daily Routine

1. **Pre-Market (30 mins)**
   - Review overnight news
   - Check futures
   - Scan for gappers
   - Review watchlist

2. **Market Hours**
   - Monitor positions
   - Look for new setups
   - Track volume patterns
   - Read company news

3. **Post-Market (1-2 hours)**
   - Chart review
   - Scan for patterns
   - Research new ideas
   - Update watchlists

### Information Sources

1. **Free Resources**
   - SEC filings (especially insider transactions)
   - Company press releases
   - StockCharts.com
   - FinViz screener
   - Yahoo Finance

2. **Avoid**
   - CNBC and financial TV
   - Analyst recommendations
   - Message boards (mostly)
   - Consensus opinions

### Pattern Recognition

1. **Weekly Chart Focus**
   - Primary timeframe for analysis
   - Weekly patterns more reliable
   - Clear trend identification

2. **Key Patterns**
   - Staircase/stepping patterns
   - Cup and handle
   - Flat base breakouts
   - Flag consolidations

## 7. Psychological Requirements

### Mindset Development

1. **Confidence Building**
   - Develop "cocky attitude" when trading
   - Transform into "gladiator" mentality
   - Believe in your analysis

2. **Emotional Control**
   - Accept large drawdowns
   - Don't panic sell
   - Don't revenge trade
   - Stay disciplined

3. **Patience**
   - Wait for perfect setups
   - Don't force trades
   - Stay in cash when appropriate
   - Let winners run

### Continuous Improvement

1. **Track Everything**
   - Entry/exit reasons
   - Pattern success rates
   - Emotional state
   - Market conditions

2. **Learn from Failures**
   - Document losing trades
   - Identify pattern breaks
   - Adjust criteria
   - Never repeat mistakes

## 8. Performance Expectations

### Realistic Goals

1. **Annual Targets**
   - 100-300% in strong years
   - Accept -50% drawdown years
   - Long-term CAGR: 50-100%

2. **Per Trade Expectations**
   - Winners: 50-1000%
   - Losers: -15-30%
   - Win rate: 30-40%
   - Profit factor: 3-5x

### Success Metrics

1. **Quality over Quantity**
   - 10-20 trades per year
   - 3-5 home runs
   - Minimize small losses

2. **Benchmark Beating**
   - 10x S&P 500 over time
   - Outperform in bull markets
